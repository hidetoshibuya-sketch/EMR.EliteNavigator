Perfusion & Shock Check (血液灌流・ショックサインの確認)

⚠️ SHOCK PROTOCOL ACTIVATED (ショックプロトコル発動)
🫁 Non-Rebreather Mask (NRB) ノンリブリージング・マスク
  • Flow: 10–15 LPM (minimum 8 LPM), FiO₂ 80–95%
    流量は10〜15 LPM（最小8 LPM以上）、酸素濃度は80%〜95%
  • Cylinder pressure check — replace cylinder when pressure falls below 500 psi, before it runs empty
    タンク圧が500 psiを切った場合は、完全に空になる前に新しいシリンダーと交換
  • Inflate the reservoir bag fully before placing on patient; ensure bag does not fully collapse on inhalation
    リザーバーバッグを膨らませてから装着し、吸気時に完全につぶれないことを確認
🛏 Shock Position 体位の確保（ショック体位）
  • Lay responsive patient supine, elevate legs 20–30 cm (8–12 in)
    反応のある患者は仰向けに寝かせ、足を20〜30cm高く上げる
  • DO NOT elevate legs if head, spine, leg, or pelvic injury suspected — keep patient flat
    ⚠ 頭部・脊椎・脚・骨盤に損傷がある場合は足を上げず水平に寝かせる
🔥 Maintain Body Temperature 保温
  • Place a blanket under and over the patient to maintain normal body temperature
    患者の下にも毛布を敷くなどして、正常な体温を保つ
  • Hypothermia worsens coagulopathy and shock — actively prevent heat loss
    低体温は凝固障害とショックを悪化させるため積極的に保温
🚫 NPO — Nothing By Mouth 飲食の禁止
  • Give the patient NO food or drink, even if they complain of thirst
    患者が喉の渇きを訴えても、食べ物や飲み物を一切与えない
  • May moisten lips with gauze if absolutely necessary
    どうしても必要な場合はガーゼで唇を湿らせるのみ

① CPR Action Flowchart (実施フローチャート)
1. Safety + ResponseScene safe, PPE, tap/shout. Infant: foot flick.安全確認・PPE・反応確認。乳児は足底刺激。
2. Call + AEDActivate EMS, send for AED. If alone with child/infant: 2 min CPR first when unwitnessed.119/AED要請。小児・乳児で単独かつ目撃なしは2分CPR後。
3. Breathing + Pulse ≤10 secAdult/child: carotid. Infant: brachial. No normal breathing or no pulse = CPR.10秒以内。成人/小児は頸動脈、乳児は上腕動脈。正常呼吸なし/脈なしでCPR。
4. Start CompressionsRate 100-120/min. Full recoil. Minimize pauses under 10 sec.100-120回/分。完全リコイル。中断10秒未満。
5. 30:2 or 15:2Adult: 30:2. Child/infant: 30:2 one rescuer, 15:2 two rescuers.成人30:2。小児/乳児は単独30:2、2人15:2。
6. AED Analyze Every 2 minClear patient, shock if advised, resume CPR immediately.2分毎解析。離れてショック、直ちにCPR再開。

Adult / 成人

Hands: two hands on lower half of sternum. Depth: 5-6 cm. Ratio: 30:2. Airway: BVM with O2, 1 sec breath to visible chest rise.

両手を胸骨下半分へ。深さ5-6cm。比率30:2。BVM+酸素で胸が上がる程度に1秒換気。

Child / 小児

Hands: one or two hands. Depth: about 5 cm or 1/3 chest depth. Ratio: 30:2 one rescuer, 15:2 two rescuers. Use pediatric AED pads if available.

片手または両手。深さ約5cm/胸郭1/3。単独30:2、2人15:2。小児用AEDパッドを優先。

Infant / 乳児

Technique: 2 fingers for one rescuer, 2-thumb encircling hands for two rescuers. Depth: about 4 cm or 1/3 AP chest depth. Ratio: 30:2 or 15:2.

単独は2本指、2人は胸郭包み込み両母指。深さ約4cm/胸郭前後径1/3。比率30:2または15:2。

ROSC / 自己心拍再開

Confirm pulse ≤10 sec. Maintain airway, support ventilation if RR <10, target SpO2 94-99%, keep AED pads on, monitor every 5 min, prepare for re-arrest, rapid transport.

10秒以内に脈拍確認。気道維持、RR<10は補助換気、SpO2 94-99%、AED装着継続、5分毎再評価、再心停止準備、迅速搬送。

Quality targets: switch compressors every 2 min, avoid over-ventilation, keep pauses short, continue until ROSC, ALS handoff, unsafe scene, or exhaustion. 質の目標: 2分毎交代、過換気回避、中断最小、ROSC/ALS引継ぎ/危険/疲労まで継続。
② Philips HeartStart HS1 (M5066A) Protocol (フィリップス HeartStart HS1 使用手順)
  1. Press the green ON/OFF button. Device powers up and self-tests.
    緑のON/OFFボタンを押す。電源ONとセルフテスト開始
  2. Expose chest. Dry skin if wet. Shave excessive hair.
    胸部を露出、濡れていれば拭く、体毛が濃ければ剃毛
  3. Peel pads and apply per diagram: Upper-right chest & lower-left rib side.
    パッドを剥がし図の通り装着（右上胸・左下肋骨側）
    小児用パッドは<8歳または25kg未満で使用、なければ成人用を前後に貼付
  4. Plug pads connector into the flashing port if not pre-connected.
    点滅しているコネクタにパッドコネクタを接続
  5. Stop touching the patient — let AED analyze rhythm.
    患者から離れる。AEDが心電図解析
  6. If shock advised: Loudly clear "I'm clear, you're clear, everybody clear!" → Press flashing orange Shock button.
    ショック適応時：大声で「離れろ」と確認→オレンジ点滅ボタンを押す
  7. Immediately resume CPR for 2 minutes regardless of result.
    結果に関わらず直ちに2分間のCPRを再開
  8. AED re-analyzes every 2 minutes — follow voice prompts continuously until ALS arrives.
    2分毎に再解析。ALS到着まで音声指示に従う

Care Performed / Trauma Interventions (実施した処置・外傷対応・10項目)

🩸 ① Bleeding Control — Direct Pressure(止血①: 直接圧迫)
Direct Pressure Protocol
  • Apply firm pressure with sterile gauze for at least 5–10 min without lifting to check.
  • If blood soaks through, add gauze on top — do not remove original layers.
  • Elevate the extremity above heart level if no fracture suspected.
  • If pressure fails to control bleeding → escalate to tourniquet or hemostatic gauze.
滅菌ガーゼで直接5〜10分以上圧迫、外さない。出血が浸透したらガーゼを追加。骨折がなければ患肢を心臓より高く挙上。制御できない場合はターニケット／止血ガーゼへ移行。
🩸 ② Bleeding Control — Tourniquet(止血②: ターニケット — 装着時刻を必ず記録)
⏱ TQ Applied at — · Elapsed 0:00
Tourniquet Protocol (CAT / SOFTT-W)
  • Place 2–3 inches (5–7 cm) above the wound, never on a joint.
  • Tighten until bleeding stops AND distal pulse is absent.
  • Write the application time on the tourniquet and patient's forehead (T-time).
  • Maximum field time guidance: ≤ 2 hours. Do not loosen en route to hospital.
  • If first TQ does not stop bleeding → apply second TQ just above the first.
創部の5〜7cm中枢側に、関節を避けて装着。出血停止＋末梢脈消失まで締める。装着時刻を必ず記録。最大2時間目安・搬送中は緩めない。第一弾で止まらなければ第二TQをすぐ中枢側に追加。
🩸 ③ Bleeding Control — Hemostatic Gauze(止血③: 止血ガーゼ・パッキング)
Hemostatic Gauze / Wound Packing
  • For wounds not amenable to tourniquet (junctional, neck, axilla, groin).
  • Pack tightly into the wound cavity directly against the bleeding source.
  • Hold direct pressure for 3 min (Combat Gauze / QuikClot) after packing.
  • Apply pressure dressing over the packed wound; do not remove gauze in the field.
ターニケット適応外の部位（接合部・頸・腋窩・鼠径）に使用。創腔の出血源に直接強く詰め込み、3分間圧迫。圧迫包帯で被覆。現場で抜去しない。
🦴 ④ Splint — Rigid (Board / SAM)(スプリント: 硬性副子・SAM)
Rigid Splint Protocol
  • Check CSM (Circulation / Sensation / Motor) before AND after splinting.
  • Immobilize the joint above AND below the injury.
  • Splint in the position found unless distal pulse is absent — then realign with gentle traction.
  • Pad bony prominences; secure with cravats, leave fingertips/toes visible.
固定前後で必ず CSM (循環・感覚・運動) 確認。受傷部位の上下関節を固定。脈拍触知不可なら軽い牽引で整復後固定。骨突起部はパッドで保護。指先は確認できるよう露出。
🦴 ⑤ Splint — C-Collar / Spinal Immobilization(頸椎カラー・脊柱固定・バックボード)
Spinal Motion Restriction
  • Manual in-line stabilization first — maintain until fully immobilized.
  • Size and apply C-collar; do not remove once on (except to clear airway).
  • Log-roll onto backboard with 3+ rescuers; head holder calls cadence.
  • Secure torso → pelvis → legs → head last; pad voids; reassess CSM all 4 extremities.
  • Indications: Mechanism + AMS, midline tenderness, neuro deficit, distracting injury, intoxication.
用手的頭部固定 → C-カラー装着 → 3人以上でログロール → 体幹→骨盤→脚→最後に頭の順で固定 → 4肢の CSM 再確認。気道確保時以外カラーを外さない。適応: 受傷機転+意識障害／正中圧痛／神経学的異常／注意力低下／中毒。
🔥 ⑥ Burn Care (cool, cover, burn sheet)(熱傷ケア: 冷却・被覆・バーンシート)
Burn Care Protocol
  • Stop the burning process — remove from source, brush off chemicals, remove smoldering clothing & jewelry (not stuck-on).
  • Cool with room-temperature water for ≤ 20 min total (avoid in > 20% TBSA — hypothermia risk).
  • Cover with dry sterile burn sheet; do not pop blisters; do not apply ointments.
  • Estimate TBSA: Rule of 9s (Adult) / Rule of palm (= 1%) / Lund-Browder (Peds).
  • Airway concern: facial burns, singed nasal hair, hoarseness, soot in mouth → high-flow O₂, anticipate intubation.
  • Maintain warmth (large burns), monitor for shock.
熱源を止める（化学物質は払い落とす）。常温水で20分以内冷却（広範囲熱傷では低体温リスクで短めに）。乾いた滅菌バーンシートで被覆、水疱は破らず軟膏は塗らない。TBSA は 9 の法則／手掌1%／Lund-Browder。気道熱傷疑い（顔面熱傷・鼻毛焦げ・嗄声・口腔煤）は高濃度O2＋挿管準備。
🧤 ⑦ Wound Care / Dressing / Bandage(創傷処置・包帯)
Wound Care Protocol
  • Control bleeding first (pressure → TQ if needed).
  • Irrigate gross debris with saline if available; do not delay transport for cleaning.
  • Cover with sterile dressing, secure with bandage — snug but not tight.
  • Impaled object: stabilize in place, do NOT remove (except cheek airway compromise).
  • Evisceration: cover with moist sterile dressing + occlusive layer; do NOT push organs back.
  • Amputation: wrap part in damp sterile gauze → sealed bag → on ice (not direct); transport with patient.
止血優先 → 生食で表面の異物を洗浄（搬送遅延させない）→ 滅菌ガーゼ被覆 → 適度な圧の包帯。刺入異物は固定し抜かない（頬の気道閉塞時のみ例外）。内臓脱出は湿潤ガーゼ＋密閉被覆、押し戻さない。切断指は湿潤滅菌ガーゼ→密閉袋→氷上（直接氷接触は禁止）で同送。
🌡 ⑧ Warmth / Hypothermia Prevention(保温・低体温予防)
Hypothermia Prevention
  • Place a blanket under AND over the patient. Insulate from cold ground.
  • Remove wet clothing, dry skin, then cover.
  • Trauma patients are 3× more likely to die if hypothermic on arrival — actively prevent heat loss.
  • Warm IV fluids if available; close ambulance doors quickly.
毛布を身体の下と上の両方に。冷たい地面から断熱。濡れた衣服を脱がせ拭いてから被覆。外傷患者は低体温で死亡率が3倍に上昇するため積極的保温。点滴は加温・救急車のドアは迅速に閉める。
💨 ⑨ Oxygen Administration (NRB / NC)(必要に応じて酸素投与)
Oxygen Administration
  • NRB (Non-Rebreather): 10–15 LPM (min 8), FiO₂ 80–95%. For shock, SpO₂ ≤ 95%, severe distress.
  • Nasal Cannula: 1–6 LPM, FiO₂ 24–44%. For mild hypoxia, COPD baseline.
  • BVM: if inadequate breathing or apnea — 15 LPM with reservoir.
  • Inflate NRB reservoir bag before placing on patient.
  • Replace O₂ cylinder when pressure drops below 500 psi.
NRB: 10〜15 LPM (最低8)、FiO2 80〜95% — ショック・SpO2≤95%・重度呼吸窮迫。経鼻カニューレ: 1〜6 LPM、FiO2 24〜44% — 軽度低酸素・COPD。BVM: 不十分呼吸・無呼吸 — リザーバー付15 LPM。装着前にリザーバーを膨らませる。シリンダー圧500 psi以下で交換。
🚑 ⑩ Packaging & Rapid Transport Decision(固定・迅速搬送判断)
Packaging & Transport Decision
  • Load-and-go (≤ 10 min on scene) indicators: airway compromise, severe bleeding, shock, AMS, penetrating torso wound, traumatic arrest, pelvic instability.
  • Choose destination: Level 1 trauma center if available within reasonable transport time.
  • Notify receiving facility early — MIST report (Mechanism · Injuries · Signs · Treatment).
  • Continue interventions en route — reassess every 5 min (unstable) or 15 min (stable).
緊急搬送 (現場10分以内) の適応: 気道障害・大出血・ショック・意識障害・体幹刺創・外傷性心肺停止・骨盤動揺。適切な距離内ならレベル1外傷センターへ。受入病院に MIST 通報 (受傷機転・損傷・所見・処置)。搬送中も処置継続、不安定5分／安定15分毎に再評価。

Care Performed / Medical Interventions (実施した処置・急病対応・10項目)

💨 ① Oxygen Administration (NRB / NC)(酸素投与: NRB または経鼻カニューレ)
Oxygen Administration Protocol
  • Indications: SpO₂ ≤ 94%, respiratory distress, shock, chest pain, AMS, stroke, suspected MI
  • NRB: 10–15 LPM (min 8) → FiO₂ 80–95%. Use for moderate-severe distress.
  • Nasal Cannula: 1–6 LPM → FiO₂ 24–44%. Use for mild hypoxia, COPD baseline.
  • BVM: if RR < 10 or apnea — 15 LPM with reservoir, squeeze every 5–6 sec (adult).
  • Inflate NRB reservoir bag BEFORE placing on patient.
  • Replace O₂ cylinder when pressure drops below 500 psi.
  • COPD caution: titrate to SpO₂ 88–92% to avoid suppressing hypoxic drive (but never withhold O₂ if hypoxic).
適応: SpO2 ≤ 94%、呼吸窮迫、ショック、胸痛、意識障害、脳卒中疑い。NRB 10〜15 LPM (FiO2 80〜95%)、経鼻カニューレ 1〜6 LPM (FiO2 24〜44%)、BVM は RR<10 or 無呼吸でリザーバー付15 LPM。COPD 患者は SpO2 88-92% に調整。タンク 500 psi 以下で交換。
🛏 ② Positioning (Shock / Recovery / Fowler)(体位管理: ショック体位・回復体位・半座位)
Patient Positioning Protocol
  • Shock position (Trendelenburg modified): supine, legs elevated 20–30 cm. Used for hypovolemic shock without head/spine/pelvic injury.
  • Recovery (lateral) position: unconscious patient with breathing, no trauma — place on side (preferably left lateral) to maintain airway and allow drainage.
  • Fowler / Semi-Fowler: upright 45–90° — for dyspnea, CHF, COPD, asthma. Improves breathing.
  • Tripod position: let dyspneic patient lean forward on arms — do not force supine.
  • Supine flat: for trauma, suspected spinal injury, syncope.
  • Left-lateral tilt (15°): for pregnant patients (3rd trimester) to relieve IVC compression.
ショック体位: 仰臥位＋足挙上20-30cm (頭部/脊椎/骨盤損傷除外)。回復体位: 意識なし呼吸ありの場合、左側臥位で気道確保。半座位 45-90°: 呼吸困難・CHF・COPD・喘息。三脚位は強制的に寝かせない。仰臥位: 外傷・脊損疑い・失神。妊婦 (後期) は左側に15°傾けて IVC 圧迫を解除。
💨 ③ Airway Management (OPA / NPA / Suction)(気道確保: OPA・NPA・吸引)
Airway Management Protocol
  • Manual maneuvers first: head-tilt/chin-lift (medical), jaw-thrust (trauma).
  • OPA (Oropharyngeal): ONLY for unconscious patient with no gag reflex. Size = corner of mouth to angle of jaw. Insert upside-down, rotate 180° at hard palate.
  • NPA (Nasopharyngeal): for semi-conscious or patients with gag reflex. Size = nostril to earlobe. Lubricate with water-soluble gel; insert with bevel toward septum. Contraindicated in suspected basilar skull fracture (CSF leak, raccoon eyes, Battle sign).
  • Suction: rigid Yankauer catheter, max 15 sec per pass (10 sec for child, 5 sec for infant). Pre-oxygenate before suctioning.
用手気道確保: 医学的は頭部後屈顎先挙上、外傷は下顎挙上。OPA: 意識なし＋咽頭反射なしのみ。サイズ=口角〜下顎角、逆向き挿入後180°回転。NPA: 半意識・咽頭反射あり。サイズ=鼻孔〜耳朶、水溶性ジェルで潤滑、ベベルを鼻中隔へ。頭蓋底骨折疑い (髄液漏・パンダ目・バトル徴候) は禁忌。吸引: ヤンカウアー使用、成人15秒/小児10秒/乳児5秒以内、前後に酸素化。
🫁 ④ BVM Ventilation(BVM 人工呼吸)
BVM Ventilation Protocol
  • Indications: RR < 10 or > 30, apnea, agonal breathing, inadequate tidal volume, CPA.
  • Equipment: appropriately sized mask, reservoir bag, 15 LPM O₂.
  • Seal: EC clamp technique (1-rescuer) or 2-handed thumbs-down (2-rescuer, preferred).
  • Rate: Adult 10–12/min (1 every 5–6 sec) · Child/Infant 12–20/min (1 every 3–5 sec) · CPR with advanced airway: 10/min continuous compressions.
  • Volume: deliver just enough to see visible chest rise (~500 mL adult). Avoid over-ventilation.
  • Avoid hyperventilation — increases intrathoracic pressure, reduces venous return.
適応: RR<10 or >30、無呼吸、努力呼吸、低換気、CPA。マスクサイズに合うリザーバー付15 LPM。1人=EC法、2人=両手親指下押し (推奨)。換気回数: 成人10-12/分、小児/乳児12-20/分、高度気道確保下は10/分連続圧迫。胸の上がりが見える程度の量 (約500mL)。過換気は胸腔内圧上昇で静脈還流低下、絶対回避。
⚡ ⑤ AED Applied(AED 装着・解析)
AED Application Protocol
  • Power ON immediately upon arrival. Expose chest (cut clothing if needed).
  • Dry skin if wet. Shave chest hair only if pad contact is poor.
  • Pad placement (Adult): upper right chest below clavicle + lower left mid-axillary line.
  • Pad placement (Peds < 8y or < 25kg): use pediatric pads if available; place ANT-POST (chest + back). If only adult pads → use adult pads ANT-POST.
  • During analysis: ALL clear, no contact with patient.
  • Shock advised: shout "I'm clear, you're clear, everybody clear!" → press button → immediately resume CPR.
  • Resume CPR for 2 minutes after every shock or "no shock advised" without checking pulse.
  • Special cases: remove transdermal patches (NTG, fentanyl); pads at least 1 inch from pacemaker bulge.
電源ON即時。胸部露出 (衣服切る)。濡れた皮膚は拭く。胸毛は接着不良時のみ剃毛。成人パッド: 右鎖骨下＋左中腋窩線下部。小児 (<8歳 or <25kg): 小児用前後貼付、なければ成人用前後貼付。解析中は全員離れる。ショック適応: 「離れろ」と確認後ボタン、即CPR再開。CPR 2分後に再解析、脈拍確認はしない。経皮パッチ (ニトロ・フェンタニル) は除去、ペースメーカー隆起から2.5cm以上離す。
💊 ⑥ Medication Assistance(投薬の補助 — 患者の処方薬・市販薬の服用補助)
⚠ Verify 3 points before assisting
EMR-Allowed Assist Medications
  • Nitroglycerin (chest pain): only if SBP ≥ 100, no Viagra/Cialis in past 24-48 h. SL spray or 0.4 mg tablet, repeat every 5 min × 3 max.
  • EpiPen (anaphylaxis): outer thigh (vastus lateralis) through clothing OK, hold 3 sec, massage area.
  • Inhaler (asthma): shake, exhale, slow deep inhale + actuate, hold breath 10 sec.
  • Oral glucose (low BG): only if conscious + able to swallow.
EMR が補助できる薬: ニトロ (SBP≥100、PDE5阻害薬24-48h以内不可、5分毎×最大3回)、エピペン (大腿外側、衣服越しOK、3秒押し、揉む)、吸入薬 (振る→呼出→ゆっくり吸入＋押す→10秒息止め)、経口ブドウ糖 (意識ありで嚥下可能のみ)。
🍬 ⑦ Oral Glucose (conscious diabetic)(経口ブドウ糖: 意識のある低血糖患者)
Oral Glucose Administration Protocol
  • Indications: conscious patient with suspected/known hypoglycemia (BG < 70 mg/dL or symptoms — diaphoresis, AMS, tremor in diabetic), able to swallow + protect airway.
  • Contraindications: AMS to point of swallow risk, unconscious, no gag reflex, suspected stroke (NPO).
  • Dose: 15 g oral glucose gel (or 4 oz juice / 3-4 glucose tablets). Apply gel between cheek and gum or have patient ingest.
  • Reassess in 15 min: if symptoms persist, repeat dose. "Rule of 15" (15 g → wait 15 min → recheck).
  • If patient becomes unresponsive: stop, protect airway, transport rapidly.
適応: 意識あり＋嚥下可能＋低血糖疑い (BG<70mg/dL、糖尿病既往＋発汗・意識障害・震え)。禁忌: 意識障害で嚥下リスク、無意識、咽頭反射なし、脳卒中疑い (NPO)。量: ブドウ糖ゼリー15g (またはジュース120mL/錠剤3-4個)、頬と歯肉の間に塗布。15分後再評価し症状残れば再投与 (Rule of 15)。意識消失したら中止・気道保護・迅速搬送。
❄ ⑧ Cooling (heat illness / fever)(冷却処置: 熱中症・高熱)
Active Cooling Protocol
  • Heat Stroke (core ≥ 40 °C + AMS): medical emergency. Cool RAPIDLY to ≤ 39 °C target.
  • Move to cool environment immediately. Remove restrictive clothing.
  • Cold water immersion (cold tub) = gold standard for exertional heat stroke if available.
  • Field alternatives: ice packs to neck, axilla, groin, wrists. Wet skin + fanning. Cold IV fluids if ALS.
  • Heat Exhaustion (no AMS): rest, oral hydration if alert (sip cool water), cool environment, light cooling.
  • STOP cooling when core reaches 38–39 °C to avoid overshoot hypothermia.
  • Monitor for shivering — defeats cooling; ALS may give IV midazolam.
熱中症 (深部≥40℃＋意識障害): 緊急、深部温≤39℃まで急速冷却。涼しい場所、衣服除去。冷水浸漬が労作性熱中症のゴールドスタンダード。現場代替: 頸・腋窩・鼠径・手首に氷嚢、皮膚を濡らして扇風、冷却IV液 (ALS)。熱疲労 (意識正常): 安静、経口冷水補液、涼所、軽い冷却。深部38-39℃で冷却中止 (オーバーシュート低体温回避)。震え発生時は冷却効果消失、ALS でミダゾラム IV。
🌡 ⑨ Warming (hypothermia / shock prevention)(保温: 低体温・ショック予防)
Warming / Hypothermia Management
  • Mild hypothermia (32–35 °C, shivering present): passive rewarming — remove wet clothes, dry skin, blankets above & below, warm environment, warm sweet drinks if alert.
  • Moderate-Severe hypothermia (< 32 °C, no shivering, AMS): handle very gently (rough movement can trigger V-fib), no oral intake, active external warming on trunk only (axilla, groin, neck), warm humidified O₂ if ALS.
  • Avoid limb rewarming first — causes cold blood return to core ("afterdrop").
  • Pulse check: palpate for 30–60 sec; if < 30 °C and pulseless, start CPR but warm before declaring death ("not dead until warm and dead").
  • Shock prevention: all trauma/shock patients need blanket UNDER and OVER — even in warm weather.
軽症低体温 (32-35℃, 震えあり): 受動的復温 — 濡れた衣服除去、皮膚乾燥、毛布上下、温環境、意識あれば温かい甘い飲料。中等度〜重症 (<32℃, 震えなし, 意識障害): 慎重に扱う (粗い動きで VF 誘発)、経口禁、体幹のみ能動加温 (腋窩・鼠径・頸)、温加湿酸素 (ALS)。四肢から温めない (冷血液が中心へ "afterdrop")。脈拍確認は30-60秒。「温めるまで死亡判定しない」原則。ショック予防: 外傷・ショック患者は暖かい日でも毛布を上下に。
🚑 ⑩ Packaging & Transport Decision(搬送準備・搬送判断)
Transport Decision (Medical)
  • Time-critical conditions (load-and-go): STEMI · stroke (within window) · severe sepsis · anaphylaxis · status epilepticus · severe respiratory distress · suspected acute abdomen with shock.
  • Destination selection:
    • STEMI → PCI-capable cardiac center (door-to-balloon < 90 min)
    • Stroke → certified stroke center (LKW < 4.5 h for tPA, < 24 h for thrombectomy)
    • Sepsis → ED with sepsis pathway
    • Trauma → Level 1 trauma center (if available)
  • Pre-arrival notification: use SBAR or MIST format. Code activation for STEMI/Stroke when criteria met.
  • Reassess en route: unstable every 5 min, stable every 15 min.
  • Position for transport: Fowler/Semi-Fowler for dyspnea, supine for shock, recovery for unconscious with airway concerns.
緊急搬送適応: STEMI・脳卒中 (時間内)・重症敗血症・アナフィラキシー・てんかん重積・重度呼吸窮迫・ショック疑い急性腹症。搬送先: STEMI→PCI 可能心臓センター (door-to-balloon<90分)、脳卒中→脳卒中センター (LKW tPA<4.5h、血栓回収<24h)、敗血症→セプシスパスウェイ ED、外傷→レベル1外傷センター。事前通報: SBAR / MIST。STEMI/Stroke コード発動。再評価: 不安定5分・安定15分毎。搬送体位: 呼吸困難は半座位、ショックは仰臥、意識障害は回復体位。
